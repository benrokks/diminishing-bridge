How to play

The ladder. Hands shrink one card per round down to a single card, then grow back to where they started. With 10 players that is 5·4·3·2·1·2·3·4·5.

Trump. After the deal, one card is flipped. Its suit is trump for the round. If that suit was already trump last round, the round is played with no trump.

Bidding. Everyone secretly bids how many tricks they will take, then all bids are revealed at once. Nobody sees a bid before committing their own.

Following suit. You must play the led suit if you hold it. Only when void may you play anything else. Any trump beats any non-trump — the 2 of trump beats the ace of the led suit.

Scoring. Hit your bid exactly for 10 + your bid. Go over and you score 1 per trick taken. Fall short and you score nothing.

The blind round. On the single-card round you can see everyone else's card but not your own.
